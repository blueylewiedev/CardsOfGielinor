# Changelog

What changed in each version of Cards of Gielinor. The newest version is first. The lines under a version are what goes in
the changelog box when that version is uploaded to mod.io, one change per line.

## 1.0.1

- 357 new NPC cards, almost all in Asgarnia, which now has 946 cards (it had 593).
- Seven cards that were the same creature in several places (Hellhound, Gorak, Glacyte, Wall beast, Rabbit, Stray dog) are now one card with variants, so the deck grows by 350 cards, to 5,686.
- The Spiritual mage, ranger and warrior have a card for each god's version.
- New roles: cook, skill trainer, emissary and archaeology collector. The Cooks, Bankers, Guards and Skill trainers sets now take in every NPC with that role.
- New sets: God emissaries and Archaeology collectors.
- The Goblins set is now the goblins of Goblin Village, Lumbridge, Draynor, the God Wars Dungeon, Bandos's stronghold and Yu'biusk, and the Wizards set is now the Wizards' Tower.
- Shorter titles on four cards whose names did not fit the card.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Earn credits from non-combat XP, Slayer kills and tasks, and boss kills.
- Open packs: a Standard pack, a Free to Play pack, a Gielinor pack and a pack for each of eleven regions.
- More than 5,000 NPC and item cards, each showing the real in-game model.
- Five rarities (common to legendary) and three finishes (normal, foil, holo).
- Collection window: search, sort and filter in a list or a grid.
- Sets window: collect groups of related cards.
- Statistics window and a credits overlay.
- In-game tutorial, opened from the plugin's ribbon page.
- Optional trackingAccess (saves against your character id) and optional httpAccess (card updates).
